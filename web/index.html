    THE VALLEY GUIDE

    Help

    Choose a sector or topic. Open a building for current costs, recipes, and requirements.

    Start here
    1. Build a woodcutter near trees.
    2. Build a farm or fishing hut near water.
    3. Add a cottage. Assign workers to your buildings.
    4. Leave an idle settler or Builder free for construction.
    5. Keep food and housing available. Select your hall for the next milestone.

    Production sectors

    Grain & crops
    Grain chain
    BuildingMakes
    Farm + acresWheat
    MillWheat → flour
    BakeryFlour + wood → bread
    • Build acres from a farm. Choose the crop in the farm panel.
    • Farmers plow all linked acres before seeding. They finish the field cycle before repeating.
    • Each crop sprouts for 15 seconds, needs one watering, then grows for 15 seconds.
    • Farmers fetch water. Keep nearby water or a well accessible.
    • Base harvest: 4.8 vegetables or 10 other crops per acre. Bonuses apply.
    • Assign a worker at every stage. Mills and bakeries each use one worker; equipment improves throughput.
    • Wheat and flour are ingredients. Bread feeds residents. Harvest becomes usable after delivery.
    • Moving or destroying a farm affects all linked acres. Farm upgrades also apply to its acres.

    Wood
    Wood chain
    BuildingMakes
    WoodcutterTrees → wood
    SawpitWood → planks
    WorkshopPlanks → tools and woodcraft
    • Place a woodcutter within 10 tile steps of trees. A diagonal counts as two steps.
    • Assign workers at each stage. Keep routes and output storage clear.
    • Forest trees regrow when the tile stays empty. Grassland trees stay cleared.
    • Wood also fuels bakeries, smokehouses, and metal production.
    • Give matching workers better tools through Items.
    • In the Workshop, choose an item and quantity, then Add to queue. Workers craft requests in order and stop when the queue is empty. Recipes marked Decoration make benches, carved statues, lanterns, flower boxes, tables, signposts, and garden arches. After delivery, place them through Build → Decorations, then use Facing to rotate them.

    Fish
    Fish chain
    BuildingRole
    Fishing hutCatch fish from nearby water.
    Fish pondUse fodder to produce fish.
    SmokehouseUse raw fish and wood to make smoked fish.
    • Place the first fishing hut within 3 tile steps of water.
    • Assign fishers. Water supplies regenerate.
    • Buy a boat at its hut to extend fishing reach. Select the boat for cargo, crew, and upgrades.
    • Smoking keeps the fish species. Each fish gains 0.5 food value and 1 coin in sale value.
    • Raw and smoked fish have separate storage allowances. Keep room for both.
    Animals & horses
    Barn growth without keepers
    AnimalBase growth
    Chicken60 seconds
    Sheep120 seconds
    Pig240 seconds
    Cow360 seconds
    • Use barn sliders to set animal counts within shared capacity.
    • Barn animals need no feed or workers to grow. Each keeper adds 1× base growth speed.
    • Eggs, wool, and milk arrive during growth. Storage and production caps still apply.
    • Adults stop producing. Select an adult at a butcher to collect and process it.
    • A free young replacement appears only when none of that species remain in the same barn. Setting its count to zero leaves it unstocked.
    • The barn shows growth progress and average cycle times. Timers wait for confirmed game updates.

    Horses

    • At hall level 8, lead a wild horse to a finished stable. Stand beside it to settle, saddle, or return horses.
    • Feed horses fodder or vegetables. Settling and growth take 5 minutes of full feeding.
    • Breed two adults with the displayed supplies. Stables hold four horses per building level.
    • A saddle gives 75% more land speed. Road bonuses also apply. Keep the saddle for later rides.
    • Return a riding horse to use it for breeding.

    Managing your town

    Resources & storage
    Reading Resources
    ColumnMeaning
    Stock (all)Stored, carried, reserved, and pending goods. Counted once.
    FreeAvailable to use now. Pending output becomes free after delivery.
    ReservedCommitted to processing or sale.
    Production capProduction target. Blank is unlimited; 0 pauses production. Does not require a market.
    FoodEdible stock in food-value units. Other rows count items.
    In/min · Out/minProduction, use, and trade in the last 60 seconds of running game time. Hauling and reservation are excluded.
    • Each resource has its own capacity. Select a building to see its stock and limits.
    • Full output storage pauses production. Processors also hold input buffers.
    • Storehouse haulers collect output and deliver ingredients. Producers also fetch inputs.
    • Enable resources in a storehouse to accept deliveries. Disabled goods already stored stay usable.
    • Use category checkboxes for a group. Option/Alt-click a resource checkbox to toggle all shown goods.
    • Storage upgrades and worker capacity upgrades are separate.
    Production caps
    Resources → Production cap
    SettingEffect
    BlankUnlimited production.
    0Pause production.
    A numberPause when stock reaches this target.
    • Changes save automatically.
    • Stored goods and output awaiting delivery count toward the cap. Reserved processor ingredients do not.
    • Production resumes as goods are used or reserved.
    • An entire recipe or animal-product batch must fit before it starts.
    Markets & trade
    Resources → trade thresholds
    SettingEffect
    Buy atBuy when free stock is below this target.
    Sell atSell surplus while retaining this amount.
    BlankDisable the rule.
    • Automatic trade needs a completed market and merchant. Changes save automatically.
    • Set Sell at higher than Buy at.
    • Merchants buy up to 10 items every 3 seconds, at twice the sale price. Coins and enabled storage limit purchases.
    • Bought goods arrive in storage. They do not advance production milestones.

    Other market controls

    • Automatic sales: enable products and set sale caps. Merchants collect and sell batches when stock reaches a cap. Older rules keep their trigger until edited in Resources.
    • Quick sale: choose a resource and amount. Review stock and the coin return, then sell.
    • Town trade: both owners need markets. Choose the town, goods, and amounts. Stock and incoming storage are checked on acceptance. Offers can be accepted once; senders can cancel them.
    Villagers, jobs & tools
    • Villagers: compare skills, talents, housing, and work status. Assign jobs here or use Add worker at a workplace.
    • Reassigning moves a worker from the old workplace. Kick removes the assignment.
    • Work earns practice. Specialists and advanced recipes need training; controls show exact requirements.
    • The hall houses your founder. Other settlers use cottages or workplaces. Resting settlers can help build.
    • Items: select a villager to give or transfer a tool. Choose Nobody to return it. Matching workers appear first.
    • Each villager carries one tool. Its bonus applies only to the matching job. Hover or focus its icon for details.
    • Locals give or sell tools. Equip your own clothing and travel gear in You.
    • Food and housing shortages can make villagers leave in normal worlds.
    Building & upgrades
    • Open Build. Choose a building, then click a clear tile. Press Esc to cancel.
    • Placement previews show valid sites and working ranges. Trees, water, rocks, buildings, and barn pens reserve space.
    • New buildings normally need 30 seconds of builder work. Idle settlers and Builders help.
    • Select a building to assign workers or preview upgrades. Benefits apply after construction finishes.
    • Your hall shows the building range. Moving the hall does not extend the territory around your original town site.
    • Moving normally costs one coin when available. Destroying gives no refund.
    • Wells supply crop water without workers. Keep an adjacent tile accessible.
    • Roads are free and take 10 seconds of builder work per tile. Walking is 50% faster on roads.
    • In Build, choose a building and use Facing · Free to rotate its preview before placement. The chosen direction stays selected for repeated placements. Completed buildings also offer Facing to rotate them and their footprints. Turns require clear ground; direction is saved when moved or reloaded. Decorations give no production bonus. Barns also offer a separate fence orientation.

    Exploration & worlds

    Exploration, locals & quests
    • Valley locates towns and wilderness villages. Walk between them. Only founders manage their own towns.
    • Visit Reedhaven, Sunwell, and Frostbrook. Stand beside locals and click them or use Interact.
    • Gifts are once per town. Trades restock after five minutes of running game time.
    • Equip swamp boots for the darkest marsh. Desert and ice need no special gear.
    • Collect nearby island treasure once per world.
    • Accept stories from Moss, Tobin, and Flint. Review objectives and keepsakes in You.
    • For Flint’s survey, examine three different rock outcrops from their panels.

    Worlds, saving & sandbox
    • The host creates and opens worlds in Worlds. Friends join the active world.
    • Use your player name and four-digit PIN to return. New players also name their village.
    • This browser remembers your login token, never your PIN. Use separate browser profiles for different founders.
    • Terrain, towns, positions, and progress save. Inactive worlds pause. Keep the host running to play.
    • Destroy world permanently deletes every town and all progress. Type its name to confirm.

    Sandbox rules

    • Start at hall level 10. All buildings and crops are unlocked.
    • Building, moving, roads, acres, and upgrades are free and instant.
    • Cottages fill automatically. Workers need no training and never leave for shortages.
    • Production inputs, hauling, storage, food consumption, animals, talents, and practice still apply.
    • Regenerate world resets that sandbox’s towns and progress. Names and PINs stay. Founders reconnect to fresh villages; other worlds are untouched.

    FLINT’S CHARCOAL MAP · PUZZLE PROTOTYPE

    The Sealed Quarry

    Two founders enter together. Hold the floor plate while a companion crosses and secures the gate. Read the inscription beyond it to open the rune seal.

    P: floor plate · G: gate · ☀ / ♣ / ☾: runes · I: inscription · T: chest. Your marker is gold. Use your movement keys or the direction buttons to walk one tile. Stand on or beside an object to use it.

    Closing this map keeps you in the expedition; reopen it from You. Leave expedition to return to the valley. Progress survives reloads. If a companion leaves, a safety counterweight lets the remaining player finish.

     COOPERATIVE COURIER PATH

    The Falling Lanterns

    A courier lost a satchel beneath the ruined lantern arches. Two founders must enter and reach the far end together. Dodge falling debris in real time using your movement keys or the buttons.

    !: warning · ×: falling debris · C: safe checkpoint · T: satchel. Gold marks you. Hits return you to your checkpoint without losing resources. Hazards pause when fewer than two founders are inside.

    Closing the map leaves the expedition. Positions and checkpoints survive rejoining and reloads. Both founders must stay inside to move. One expedition per world; each member earns 25 coins and a courier lantern badge once.
