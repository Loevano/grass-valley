    Production caps

    Quick help

    Click anything on the map for details.

    Start here

    Start with a woodcutter near trees, a farm or fishing hut near water, and a cottage. Assign villagers to work.

    Keep 20 Food, then select your hall for the first upgrade.

    Controls

    WASD walk · F fish · C chop · G gather. Work beside a source; move or press the key again to stop. I interacts with the nearest local beside you, opening dialogue and trade.

    Scroll to pan; pinch or Alt/Option-scroll to zoom. Y toggles camera follow.

    1 Worlds · 2 Build · 3 Villagers · 4 Resources · 5 Items · 6 Valley · 7 Help · 8 Rules. Esc closes or cancels. Change keys in Settings.

    Building

    Open Build, choose a building, then click a clear tile. Esc cancels.

    Woodcutters need trees within 10 steps (a diagonal counts as two); farms and fishing huts need water within 3 steps. Build acres from a farm.

    New buildings need 30 seconds of builder work. Keep an idle villager or Builder available.

    Jobs & upgrades

    Assign jobs in Villagers, or select a workplace and choose Add settler.

    Specialists need practice in an earlier job. Check the workplace for requirements and upgrades; select the hall for your next milestone.

    Production
    • Wood → sawpit → planks → workshop → tools.
    • Wheat → mill → flour → bakery → bread.
    • Fish → smokehouse → smoked fish.
    • Vegetables or fodder → barn → animal goods; meat → butcher shop.

    Assign workers at each stage. Select buildings for recipes. Edible goods count as Food; wheat and flour are ingredients. Markets sell surplus goods.

    Work stopped?

    Check the worker’s status: finish construction, check source reach, train required skills, or supply ingredients and feed.

    Storage full? Spend goods or build a storehouse. Trees and wild fish regenerate; add acres or animals when fields or barns are fully tended.

    FLINT’S CHARCOAL MAP · PUZZLE PROTOTYPE

    The Sealed Quarry

    Two founders enter together. Hold the floor plate while a companion crosses and secures the gate. Read the inscription beyond it to open the rune seal.

    P: floor plate · G: gate · ☀ / ♣ / ☾: runes · I: inscription · T: chest. Your marker is gold. Use your movement keys or the direction buttons to walk one tile. Stand on or beside an object to use it.

    Closing this map keeps you in the expedition; reopen it from You. Leave expedition to return to the valley. Progress survives reloads. If a companion leaves, a safety counterweight lets the remaining player finish.

     COOPERATIVE COURIER PATH

    The Falling Lanterns

    A courier lost a satchel beneath the ruined lantern arches. Two founders must enter and reach the far end together. Dodge falling debris in real time using your movement keys or the buttons.

    !: warning · ×: falling debris · C: safe checkpoint · T: satchel. Gold marks you. Hits return you to your checkpoint without losing resources. Hazards pause when fewer than two founders are inside.

    Closing the map leaves the expedition. Positions and checkpoints survive rejoining and reloads. Both founders must stay inside to move. One expedition per world; each member earns 25 coins and a courier lantern badge once.
