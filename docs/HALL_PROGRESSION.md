# Hall progression

Each upgrade proves a production chain available at the current hall level before
unlocking the next set of buildings. Production requirements count lifetime output
made by this town, even after the goods are eaten, sold, or used. Imported goods
can pay the material investment but do not count as local production. Materials
are spent when construction starts; coins are awarded once when it finishes.

| Upgrade | Development required at the current level | Hall investment | Builder work | Main unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 2 | Completed cottage and a completed farm or fishing hut within 10 tile steps of natural water or a completed well; at least 20 food | 40 wood | 30 seconds | Wheat, barn, chickens, fishing nets |
| 2 → 3 | Harvest 20 wheat and produce 6 eggs | 50 wood | 60 seconds | Sawpit, planks, sheep, carrots, fodder, boats |
| 3 → 4 | Produce 20 planks | 60 wood + 15 planks | 90 seconds | Mill, bakery, smokehouse, workshop, butcher, pigs |
| 4 → 5 | Craft 5 tools and produce 10 processed food items, in any mix | 70 wood + 20 planks + 5 tools | 120 seconds | Tannery, market, fish pond, orchard, cows, timber framing |
| 5 → 6 | Produce 6 leather | 80 wood + 25 planks + 4 leather | 150 seconds | Mine |
| 6 → 7 | Mine 20 iron ore | 90 wood + 30 planks + 5 tools | 180 seconds | Forgery (smelter) |
| 7 → 8 | Smelt 10 iron | 100 wood + 35 planks + 10 iron | 210 seconds | Stable, saddler, horses |
| 8 → 9 | Craft 2 saddles | 110 wood + 40 planks + 10 iron + 5 leather | 240 seconds | Steelworks, steel pickaxes |
| 9 → 10 | Produce 15 steel and complete one town trade or sell 10 goods at a market | 120 wood + 45 planks + 10 steel | 270 seconds | Trade house |

Processed food includes every bakery and butcher recipe plus smoked fish of any
species. Flour, raw fish, and raw meat do not qualify. Tools and food are both
required, so either the grain, fishing, or livestock branch can complement the
workshop. Barns and wheat introduce husbandry and farming before the leather chain.

Wood rises by 10 per upgrade; once sawpits are available, planks rise by 5.
Tools fund workshop and mining development; leather supplies fittings and iron
and steel reinforce the later hall. Wheat, eggs, and processed food remain for
feeding residents. The ore milestone keeps all ore available for smelting rather
than consuming it before the Forgery unlock. Two saddles prove repeat production
and remain available for riding; capturing, settling, and riding a horse are
optional activities. The final upgrade leaves five of its required fifteen steel
for equipment or saving toward the trade house's ten-steel construction cost.

Buildings and their existing costs are unchanged. Workers still need the normal
individual profession practice before operating a newly unlocked building.
Larger timber investments require storage beyond the hall's 100-wood allowance.
Construction durations and coin rewards retain their existing progression.
Existing completed hall levels and already-paid upgrades are preserved on reload.
Sandbox worlds continue to skip milestones and costs.
