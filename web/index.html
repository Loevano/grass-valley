    THE VALLEY GUIDE

    Help

    Open a topic for instructions. Select objects on the map for their current status, costs, and requirements.

    Start here

    Build a woodcutter near trees, a farm or fishing hut near water, and a cottage. Assign villagers to work and leave an idle settler or Builder available for construction.

    Keep food and housing available so villagers stay. Select your hall to see the next milestone, its rewards, and upgrade requirements. Hall upgrades unlock new buildings and crops.

    Build places buildings; Villagers manages jobs; Resources tracks stock and trade; Items assigns tools; Valley locates towns and wilderness villages; Rules sets production caps.

    Controls & camera

    W/A/S/D walk · Y camera lock · F fish · C chop · G gather · M clear stone · B building mode · I interact.

    Click the map to focus it, then use your movement keys to walk. Work beside a source; move or press its action key again to stop. Interact opens a nearby local’s dialogue and trade. Clearing a grassland rock removes it permanently and needs room for its full stone yield.

    Scroll to pan; pinch or Alt/Option-scroll to zoom. Camera lock follows your player. Space centers on your town hall. Arrow keys choose a map tile; Enter inspects it. Click objects to inspect them; overlapping objects offer a selection list.

    1 Worlds · 2 Build · 3 Villagers · 4 Resources · 5 Items · 6 Valley · 7 Help · 8 Rules · ⌘, Settings. Press a window’s number again to close it. Esc closes or cancels. Change gameplay keys in Settings; the bindings above follow your changes.

    Building & upgrades

    Open Build, choose a building, and click a clear tile. Trees, water, rocks, and other buildings reserve their tiles. Placement previews show valid sites and working ranges. Esc cancels.

    Woodcutters need trees within 10 horizontal or vertical tile steps; a diagonal counts as two. Farms and fishing huts need nearby water, initially within 3 steps. Wells provide crop water without assigned workers; keep an adjacent tile accessible.

    New buildings normally need 30 seconds of builder work. Idle villagers and Builders help construct them. Select a building to assign workers and preview upgrades, costs, work time, and requirements. Benefits apply when construction finishes. Worker capacity and storage upgrades are separate; mill and bakery equipment increases production with one worker.

    Your hall shows the building range. Construction must also stay near the original town site, so moving the hall does not extend your territory. Moving normally costs one coin when available. Destroying gives no refund. Moving or destroying a farm also moves or removes its linked acres. Farm upgrades apply to all linked acres. Barns reserve a fenced pen; choose an available fence orientation in their panel.

    Roads are free, need 10 seconds of builder work per tile, and make players and settlers walk 50% faster. Decorations provide no production bonus.

    Villagers, jobs & tools

    Compare skills, talents, housing, and work status in Villagers. Assign jobs there or select a workplace and choose Add worker. Reassignment moves the worker from their previous workplace; Kick removes the assignment.

    Specialists need practice in earlier jobs. Workplace controls show exact requirements; working builds practice, unlocking recipes and improving production. Talent affects efficiency. The hall houses your controllable founder; other settlers live in cottages or workplaces. Housing and storage benefits are automatic, and resting settlers can help build.

    Items assigns and transfers worker tools. Select a villager to equip a tool or Nobody to return it to inventory. Matching workers appear first. Each villager carries one tool; its output bonus applies only to its matching job. Hover or focus equipment icons to read the bonus.

    Collect gift tools from locals such as Sylva, Reed, and Fern; buy stronger tools from toolsmiths or find equipment through treasure and trades. Equip your own clothing and travel gear in the You panel. Keep food and housing available: shortages can make villagers leave in normal worlds.

    Resources & storage

    Resources compares total stock, free stock, reserved goods, trade thresholds, and activity. Stock includes stored goods, workplace output, reserved ingredients, and carried goods, counted once. Free is usable now; Reserved is committed to processing or sale. Pending output becomes free after delivery.

    Food summarizes edible goods in food-value units; individual resource rows count items. Residents consume edible goods. Wheat and flour are ingredients rather than food. In/min and Out/min record actual production, use, purchases, and sales over the last 60 seconds of running game time; internal hauling and reservation do not count.

    Each resource has its own storage capacity. Select a building for stock and capacity by resource. Production buffers and processor input buffers hold goods awaiting work or delivery; full output storage pauses production. Storage upgrades raise limits independently of worker capacity.

    Storehouse haulers collect finished output and deliver processor inputs; producers also fetch required ingredients. Enable accepted resources in a storehouse. Disabled goods already stored remain usable. Category checkboxes toggle their shown resources; Option/Alt-click a resource checkbox to toggle all shown resources. Unlocks reveal more resources while existing stock stays visible.

    Production & farming
    • Wood → sawpit → planks → workshop → tools.
    • Wheat → mill → flour → bakery → bread.
    • Fish → smokehouse → smoked fish of the same species, adding 0.5 food and 1 coin per fish.
    • Iron vein → mine → iron ore → forgery → iron → advanced metal goods.
    • Growing barn animals → eggs, wool, or milk; adults → butcher → meat and hides → prepared meat.

    Assign workers at each stage and select production buildings for recipes. Recipe rows show ingredients, output, batch time, practice, food value, and required worker level. Skilled workers can make larger batches with matching ingredient demand.

    Build acres from a farm and choose its crop for all linked acres. Acres start unplowed. Farmers finish every acre’s cycle before repeating and plow all linked acres before seeding. Crops sprout for 15 seconds, need one watering with a water trip, then grow for 15 more seconds. Base harvest is 4.8 vegetables or 10 other crops per acre before bonuses; goods become available after delivery.

    Forest trees regrow if their tile stays clear; grassland trees stay cleared. Water supplies regenerate. Fishing boats extend reach; buy a boat at its hut, then select the boat on the map for cargo, crew, and upgrades.

    Production caps

    Open Rules to pause production at a resource target. Leave blank for unlimited; use 0 to pause. Changes save automatically.

    Stored goods and output awaiting delivery count toward the cap. Reserved processor ingredients do not, so construction and upgrade stock can replenish. Production resumes as goods are used or reserved. Recipe and animal product batches wait until the whole batch fits.

    Markets & trade

    A completed market and assigned merchant enable automatic trade. In Resources, Buy at and Sell at use free stock; blank disables a rule. The sale threshold must exceed the buy threshold. Selling retains the threshold amount.

    Merchants buy up to 10 items every 3 seconds at twice the sale price, stopping at the free-stock target, available coins, or enabled storage. Bought goods arrive in town storage and do not count toward production milestones.

    The market’s Automatic sales controls enable products and set sale caps. When stock reaches a cap, merchants collect goods and return to sell batches. Existing market rules retain their original trigger until edited in Resources. Changes save automatically.

    Quick sale sells a chosen amount at the displayed price without changing automatic rules. Choose or type a supported resource; the form shows available stock and the coin return.

    To trade with another town, both owners need completed markets. Choose the town, goods, and quantities, then create an offer. Stock is checked when accepted; incoming goods need enabled storage. Each offer can be accepted once; the sender can cancel an open offer.

    Animals & horses

    Barn sliders set animal counts within shared capacity. Animals grow without feed or workers; each keeper adds 1× base growth speed. Base growth takes 60 seconds for chickens, 120 for sheep, 240 for pigs, and 360 for cows. The panel shows current speed, individual progress, and average cycle times; countdowns wait for confirmed game updates.

    Eggs, wool, and milk are collected during growth as storage and production caps allow. Adults stop producing and stay until manually butchered. A free young animal appears only when no others of that species remain in the same barn. Setting a species count to zero leaves it unstocked.

    Select an adult at a butcher to see its rewards and start collection and processing. The butcher fetches the animal and brings it back before processing begins. Keep routes, output storage, and production caps clear.

    At hall level 8, lead a wild horse to a finished stable. Stand beside the stable to settle, saddle, or return a horse. Horses need fodder or vegetables; settling and growth take five minutes of full feeding. Breed two adults with the displayed supplies. Stables hold four horses per building level.

    A saddle lets you ride an adult for 75% more land speed; road bonuses also apply. Your saddle stays with you for later rides. Return a riding horse to the stable to use it for breeding.

    Exploration, locals & quests

    Valley centers the camera on a town or wilderness village in the shared world. Each town belongs to its founder; only its owner manages construction and workers. Walk between towns, explore biomes, and visit Reedhaven, Sunwell, and Frostbrook.

    Walk beside a local and click them or use Interact to talk, claim gifts, or trade. Offers show costs and equipment bonuses; gifts are once per town and trades restock after five minutes of running game time. Equip swamp boots to cross the darkest marsh; desert and ice ground are walkable without special gear. Nearby island treasure can be collected once per world.

    Moss, Tobin, and Flint offer stories. Accept beside them, follow the objectives, and return with the requested supplies. Flint’s survey requires examining three different rock outcrops from their panels. Select You to review objectives and keepsakes.

    The Sealed Quarry in You opens a cooperative puzzle. Two founders enter to unlock movement. One holds the floor plate while another secures the gate, then follow the inscription’s rune order and recover the lantern stone. Close map keeps you inside; Leave expedition returns to the valley. A counterweight lets the last remaining founder finish. Completion grants each member 20 coins and a keepsake once per world.

    The Falling Lanterns is a cooperative movement quest. Two founders must stay inside; fewer pauses movement and hazards. Avoid amber warnings and red debris, use column C as a checkpoint, and reach T together. Hits return you to a checkpoint without resource loss. Closing this map leaves the expedition; rejoining preserves progress. Completion grants 25 coins and a badge once per world.

    Worlds, saving & sandbox

    The host creates and opens worlds from Worlds; friends join the active world. Use your player name and four-digit PIN to recover your village. New players also choose a village name. This browser remembers your login token, never your PIN. Separate browser profiles let different founders play on one computer.

    Terrain, towns, player positions, and progress save with the world. Inactive worlds pause; keep the host running to play. Destroy world permanently removes every town and all progress after name confirmation.

    Sandbox starts with a level-10 hall and all buildings and crops unlocked. Building, acres, roads, moving, and upgrades are free and instant. Cottages fill automatically; workers need no training and never leave for food or housing shortages. Talents, practice, production ingredients, hauling, storage, animals, and food consumption still run normally.

    The host can regenerate a sandbox with fresh terrain after name confirmation. This resets its towns and progress, preserves registered names and PINs, and reconnects founders to fresh villages with the same names. Other worlds remain saved.

    Settings

    Camera lock follows your character when on; turn it off to explore freely. Graphics Low reduces resolution, Medium uses standard resolution, and High uses full display resolution. Lower quality can help slower devices. Changes apply immediately and save in this browser.

    Select a keybinding and press a unique letter or 0. Keys 1–9 are reserved for windows; arrow keys and Enter inspect tiles. Bindings save in this browser. Restore default keybindings resets gameplay keys. Escape closes Settings.

    Work stopped?

    Read the building’s status and worker warnings. Finish construction, assign qualified workers, keep a source in reach, supply ingredients, and clear routes to workplaces, water, and storage.

    If storage is full, spend goods, enable storage, add a storehouse and hauler, or upgrade capacity. If a production cap is reached, use stock or raise it in Rules. Recipe batches need room for the whole batch.

    Fully grown barn animals wait for manual butchery and stop making products. Horses need feed. Food and housing shortages appear in the toolbar and Villagers; address them before residents leave in normal worlds.

    The Sealed Quarry

    Two founders enter together. Hold the floor plate while a companion crosses and secures the gate. Read the inscription beyond it to open the rune seal.

    P: floor plate · G: gate · ☀ / ♣ / ☾: runes · I: inscription · T: chest. Your marker is gold. Use your movement keys or the direction buttons to walk one tile. Stand on or beside an object to use it.

    Closing this map keeps you in the expedition; reopen it from You. Leave expedition to return to the valley. Progress survives reloads. If a companion leaves, a safety counterweight lets the remaining player finish.

     COOPERATIVE COURIER PATH

    The Falling Lanterns

    A courier lost a satchel beneath the ruined lantern arches. Two founders must enter and reach the far end together. Dodge falling debris in real time using your movement keys or the buttons.

    !: warning · ×: falling debris · C: safe checkpoint · T: satchel. Gold marks you. Hits return you to your checkpoint without losing resources. Hazards pause when fewer than two founders are inside.

    Closing the map leaves the expedition. Positions and checkpoints survive rejoining and reloads. Both founders must stay inside to move. One expedition per world; each member earns 25 coins and a courier lantern badge once.
