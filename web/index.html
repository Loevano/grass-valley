A gentle beginning

Click a building, villager, tree, water tile, or rock to inspect it. Close panels with Escape. You can also focus the map and use arrow keys and Enter to select tiles.

Open Build, choose a building, select open grass, then confirm with Build here. Place woodcutters within 3 horizontal or vertical tile steps of trees, and farms within 3 steps of water.

Assign jobs through Villagers or a villager's information box. Build cottages for more residents and storehouses for more storage.

Click the town hall to see milestone progress and upgrade it. Open Valley to visit another town; visitors can inspect but only owners can make changes.

Trees and water are renewable. Rocks cannot be mined yet. Adventures and magic are planned for a later version.
